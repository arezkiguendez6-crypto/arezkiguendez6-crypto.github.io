🏠 Flan Recipe

Recipe

Total time: About 4 hours 20 minutes, including chi lling (about 1 hour 20 minutes active/baking time)

Servings: 8

IngredientAmount
Granulated sugar1 cup
Water1/4 cup
Sweetened condensed milk1 can (14 ounces)
Heavy cream1 cup
Large eggs5
Large egg yolks2
Vanilla extract2 teaspoons

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
  2. Bring the sugar and water to a boil in a small saucepan. Gently swirl the pan instead of stirring.
  3. Lower the heat to medium and cook, swirling occasionally, until the syrup becomes a dark caramel color, about 12–16 minutes.
  4. Carefully pour the caramel into an 8-inch round cake pan and swirl to coat the bottom. Let it cool for about 10 minutes.
  5. Blend the condensed milk, heavy cream, eggs, egg yolks, and vanilla until smooth. Pour over the caramel and cover the pan tightly with foil.
  6. Place the cake pan inside a roasting pan. Put it in the oven and carefully add hot tap water until it reaches halfway up the sides of the cake pan.
  7. Bake for 50–60 minutes, until a tester inserted in the center comes out clean.
  8. Carefully remove the pans from the oven. Let the flan cool for at least 30 minutes, then cover and refrigerate for at least 3 hours or overnight.
  9. Run a thin knife around the edge. Place a plate over the pan and carefully flip it over to release the flan.

Tips, Tricks, and Substitutions

  • Watch the sugar carefully while making caramel so it does not b urn.
  • Blend the custard only until smooth to avoid making too much fo am.
  • Use a water bath so the custard cooks gently and evenly.
  • Let the flan cool, then refrigerate it for at least 3 hours bef ore serving.
  • Carefully run a thin knife around the edge before turning it on to a plate.
