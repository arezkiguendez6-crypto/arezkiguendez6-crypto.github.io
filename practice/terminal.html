Once we're in Vim, how are we supposed to move around?

  • h - Moves Left
  • l - Moves Right
  • j - Moves Down
  • k - Moves Up

What do specific elements/tags do?

The "pre" tag displays the text as monotone, for example.

Hello, Everyone!
This is my website
as you can see.
I made this rhyme
for you all to read!

The "code "tag" turns text inside of it to monospaced font, which is useful in paragraphs to display the code, for example.

Lorem ipsum dolo rsit amet, consetetur sadipscing elitr, sed diam nonumy eirmod tempor invidunt ut labore et dolore magna aliquyam.

How to use KBD and Samp

The "Kbd" tag sometimes envelops text with a rectanglular box and with mono tone font, for example.

Cmd + Z

The "Samp" tag is a monospaced font that can be bold or italicized that repr- esents a output, such as.

Incorrect Password Try Again.

Lastly, how to leave and save a vim project.

  • : + w to save but not leave
  • : + q to quit without saving
  • : +wq to quit and save
