🏠 Snickerdoodle Cookie Recipe

Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour (2¾ cups)
  • Cream of tartar (2 teaspoons)
  • Baking soda (1 teaspoon)
  • Salt (½ teaspoon)
  • Unsalted butter (1 cup, just softened)
  • Sugar (1½ cups)
  • Eggs (2)
  • Vanilla extract (1 teaspoon)

Cinnamon Sugar Coating

  • sugar (1/3 cup)
  • cinnamon (2 tablespoons)

Instructions

  1. In a medium bowl, stir the 2¾ cup flour, 2 teaspoons cr eam of tartar, 1 teaspoon baking soda, and ½ teaspoon salt. Set aside.
  2. In the bowl of a stand mixer (or use a hand mixer) cream togeth er 1 cup butter (barely softened) and 1½ cups sugar until light and fluffy (at least 2 minutes) Add 2 eggs and 1 teaspoon vanil la and mix until it is well blended.
  3. Add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients, mix well, and t hen shape the dough into 1-inch balls (no need to chill).
  4. In a small bowl, combine ⅓ cup sugar and 2 tablespoons cinnamon . Roll the dough balls into the cinnamon and sugar mixture twice .
  5. Place on an un-greased pan or baking sheets 2 inches apart (fla tten with the bottom of a cup if you like your cookies thinner) , and bake at 350°F for 8 to 10 minutes, or until the edges are set but the centers still look slightly underdone.

Tips & Tricks!

  • Slightly Soften your butter
  • Don't skip the tartar if you want that tangy flavor
  • Roll the balls in the cinnamon sugar mix twice
  • Don't overbake the cookies even if taken out they look s oft, they'll continue baking on the pan and become chewy
  • If the dough is sticky freeze it for about 20 minutes
